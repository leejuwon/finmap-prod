Mortgage payment calculator FAQ

How is this different from the DSR/LTV calculator?

This calculator starts from a loan amount and estimates payment and interest. The DSR/LTV calculator starts from income, existing debt, home price, cash, LTV, and DSR assumptions to estimate affordability.

Which repayment method should I use?

Equal payment is easier for monthly cash-flow planning. Equal principal starts with a higher payment, declines over time, and usually produces lower total interest.

What does the +1pp rate result mean?

It keeps the loan amount, term, and repayment type unchanged and shows how the representative monthly payment changes if the annual rate rises by one percentage point.

Does this result guarantee mortgage approval?

No. Actual terms can differ by lender review, final rate, credit profile, recognized income, regulated area, stress DSR, collateral value, and guarantee conditions.
